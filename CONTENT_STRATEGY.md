# Mesothelioma Site Content Strategy

## Goal
Build topical authority through consistent, high-quality content over 12 months.

**Target:** 3 articles/week = ~150 articles/year

## Content Pillars

### 1. State Legal Guides (50 articles)
- Deep-dive legal guides for each state
- Statute of limitations details
- State-specific asbestos regulations
- Local court information

### 2. Exposure Site Articles (40 articles)
- Detailed histories of asbestos exposure sites
- Worker testimonials/stories
- Company accountability
- Site-specific health risks

### 3. Veteran Content (25 articles)
- Navy ship asbestos exposure guides
- Military base exposure
- VA benefits guides
- Branch-specific content (Navy, Army, Air Force, Marines)

### 4. Medical/Informational (20 articles)
- Treatment options
- Diagnosis guides
- Living with mesothelioma
- Caregiver resources

### 5. Legal Process (15 articles)
- How lawsuits work
- Settlement vs trial
- Trust fund claims
- Wrongful death claims

## SEO Targets Per Article

- **Word count:** 1,500-3,000 words
- **Headers:** H2s with keyword variations
- **Internal links:** 3-5 per article
- **Schema:** Article schema on all posts
- **Meta:** Unique title/description targeting long-tail keywords
