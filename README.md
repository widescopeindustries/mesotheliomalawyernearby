# Mesothelioma Lead Generation Site

A highly lucrative, execution-dependent lead generation platform for mesothelioma legal services, built as a veteran-owned and operated business.

## 🎯 Business Model

**Pay Per Call (PPC) Revenue: $200-$500 per qualified call**
- Target high-CPC, low-competition regional keywords
- Focus on "unicorn" markets like New Mexico and Missouri
- Capture $50M+ in total addressable market

## 🛠️ Core Features Implemented

### ✅ Foundation
- Next.js 14 with TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS with modern design system
- SDVOSB trust signals prominently displayed
- Veteran-owned messaging integrated throughout

### ✅ Trust & Authority Components
- **About Page**: Complete SDVOSB certification display, veteran story
- **Trust Signals**: Badges, certifications, social proof
- **Hero Section**: Strong veteran-to-veteran messaging
- **How It Works**: Clear 4-step conversion process

### ✅ Geographic Targeting
- **Featured Locations**: High-value markets (Albuquerque, St. Louis, Houston, etc.)
- **Programmatic Architecture**: Ready for 100+ city-level pages
- **Regional Focus**: State-specific legal content
