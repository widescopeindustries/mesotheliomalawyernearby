## Tips

1. **Follow up by phone** - Email response rates are low, call 2-3 days after
2. **Verify the lead first** - Call Miller Mark yourself to confirm before selling
3. **Get pricing in writing** - Ask for their standard per-lead rate
4. **Negotiate exclusivity** - Exclusive leads sell for 2-3x more
5. **Ask about their intake criteria** - So you can qualify leads better
