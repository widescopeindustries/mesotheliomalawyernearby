# OpenCode Skills for Mesothelioma Lead Generation

To help you execute this "Sniper" strategy for your regional mesothelioma directory, we need to equip OpenCode with skills that bridge the gap between high-level data analysis and programmatic site generation.

Since you've identified those "unicorn" keywords (like New Mexico and Missouri with high CPCs and low KD), these skills are designed to turn that research into a live, high-authority lead-gen engine.

## 🛠️ Specialized OpenCode Skills

### 1. Programmatic Geo-Entity Architect
This skill enables OpenCode to map your regional keyword data to specific geographic datasets. Instead of just "Missouri," it will understand the hierarchy of cities, counties, and zip codes to create thousands of hyper-local landing pages.

**Expertise:** Mapping CPC and KD metrics to city-level subdirectories.

**Function:** Automatically generating a slug structure (e.g., /lawyers/missouri/st-louis/) and injecting local data into H1 and Meta tags.

### 2. Legal-Veteran Authority Synthesizer
Given your 20-year Army background, this skill ensures the programmatic content doesn't feel like "spam." It will blend your veteran status with legal informational content to boost E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness).

**Expertise:** Merging VA-specific terminology with Mesothelioma medical facts.

**Function:** Creating "Veteran-to-Veteran" trust sections on every page that highlight your service and provide a supportive tone for claimants.

### 3. PPC (Pay Per Call) Conversion Optimizer
Since your goal is to sell calls ($200 - $500/call), the site needs to be a conversion machine. This skill focuses on the technical placement of "Click-to-Call" assets.

**Expertise:** High-visibility UI/UX for mobile users who need immediate legal consultation.

**Function:** Generating dynamic "Call Now" buttons that track the specific city/region page the user is calling from to provide better data to your PPC network.

### 4. Asbestos Data & Regulatory Scraper
To make the site "Informational" (and satisfy Google's helpful content requirements), this skill will fetch and format regional data about known asbestos sites or local regulations.

**Expertise:** Parsing OSHA and state-level environmental records.

**Function:** Automatically creating sections like "Known Asbestos Exposure Sites in [City Name]" using your uncovered regional research.
